Voice notes till 2 AM. Morning: “In a meeting”

And radio silence until dark. The whiplash between Sunday warmth and Monday's corporate wall is disorienting: hours ago you were his closest confidante, and now you feel like an inconvenient disruption in his calendar.

On Monday mornings, many people flip into pure operational mode, putting emotional connection on ice. But being busy and respecting a relationship are not mutually exclusive. A partner who genuinely values your place in his life finds five seconds to leave a warm marker between meetings.

Don't put your entire day on hold waiting by the screen. Take your week back.
